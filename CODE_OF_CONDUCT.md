# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while evaluating
technical claims on evidence. Welcome people regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or viewpoint.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, publishing private information, or personal
attacks.

## Scope and Enforcement

These standards apply in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and
other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is not tolerated.
